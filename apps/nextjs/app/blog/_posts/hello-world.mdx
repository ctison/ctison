# Hello, world

This is the first post on `@ctison.dev`. I wanted a low-friction place to keep
engineering notes, design experiments, and the occasional longer thought.

The whole site is small on purpose: Next.js, Tailwind, and MDX. Posts are just
files, which means writing is the same as committing.

> If it's worth thinking about twice, it's worth writing down once.
