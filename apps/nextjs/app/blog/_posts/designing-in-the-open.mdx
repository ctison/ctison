# Designing in the open

The palette here is deliberately small: a near-black background, zinc for
structure, and almost nothing else. Constraints make decisions faster.

A few rules I'm trying to keep:

- One accent at a time, never two competing.
- Type scale over color for hierarchy.
- Motion only when it explains a change of state.

Dark mode isn't an afterthought — it's the default, and the light theme is the
variant. Everything is driven by a single class on `<html>`:

```css
@custom-variant dark (&:where(.dark, .dark *));
```

We'll see how well it holds up as the site grows.
